Parth Plasto Pack Pvt. Ltd. is a leading manufacturer of high-end plastic packaging solutions for the pharmaceutical, nutraceutical, and healthcare industries. With over 25 years of experience, we combine cutting-edge injection moulding technology with uncompromising quality standards.

Our state-of-the-art cleanroom facility in Ahmedabad, Gujarat produces over 500+ packaging products serving 1000+ clients across 20+ states in India and global markets.

OUR PROCESS

From Concept to Delivery

A streamlined 6-step manufacturing process ensuring quality at every stage.

💬
STEP 01
Consultation

We understand your packaging requirements, product specifications, regulatory needs, and volume projections through detailed consultation.

✏️
STEP 02
Design & Engineering

Our engineering team creates precise CAD models and technical drawings tailored to your product, considering material selection and tooling requirements.

🔬
STEP 03
Prototype Development

We produce physical prototypes for your evaluation, including stability testing samples and compatibility trials before mass production.

🏭
STEP 04
Manufacturing

State-of-the-art injection blow moulding and extrusion blow moulding in our ISO-certified cleanroom facility ensures zero-defect production.

✅
STEP 05
Quality Inspection

Rigorous multi-point quality checks including dimensional accuracy, leak testing, drop testing, and MVTR analysis on every production batch.

🚚
STEP 06
Packaging & Delivery

Secure packaging, batch documentation, and reliable logistics ensuring safe delivery across 20+ states in India and international markets.
