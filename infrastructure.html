Machinery & Plant Highlights

Explore the specialized technological pillars powering our zero-defect production.

High-Speed All-Electric Machines

Servo-driven injection moulding units from 50T to 350T ensuring sub-millimeter precision and ultra-fast cycle times.

Robotic Take-Out Automation

Integrated Cartesian pick-and-place robots for touchless part handling straight from mould cavity into cleanroom conveyors.

Automatic Desiccant Insertion

Automated cap assembly lines inserting molecular sieve desiccant chambers with airtight spiral springs.

Class 100k (ISO 8) Certified

Positive-pressure cleanroom areas conforming to international pharmaceutical packaging standards.

HEPA H14 Air Filtration

Continuous laminar airflow filtration removing airborne particulates down to 0.3 microns at 99.99% efficiency.

Airlock Entry Protocols

Strict PPE gowning, air showers, and pass-box material transit preventing cross-contamination.

Optical Dimensional Projectors

Non-contact digital vision measurement systems validating thread pitch, wall thickness, and neck diameters.

Vacuum Leak & Moisture Testers

Automated vacuum chamber leak detection guaranteeing 100% hermetic and moisture-proof seals.

Cap Torque & Drop Impact Analyzers

Calibrated digital torque testers to ensure optimal child-resistant and tamper-evident cap opening forces.

In-House Tooling & CAD/CAM

Dedicated mold design center utilizing advanced CAD modeling and high-speed CNC spark EDM equipment.

Preventative Mold Maintenance

Scheduled ultrasonic cavity cleaning and mold refurbishment protocols ensuring zero downtime.

Custom Cavity Development

Rapid engineering turnaround for custom bottle shapes, closure sizes, and embossed brand logos.
